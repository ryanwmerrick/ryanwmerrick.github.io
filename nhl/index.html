    How rankings work

    Overall is an Elo rating. Everyone starts at 1000. Beating someone rated higher than you earns more than beating someone rated lower.

    Margin counts a little. A regulation win is worth more the bigger the margin (1 + 0.5 × ln(margin), up to 2×). An OT or shootout win counts as 0.75 of a win with no margin bonus, so the loser still keeps a bit.

    Teams count a little too. Each NHL team has a hidden strength that moves slowly with results (max ±40 points). Winning with a team that keeps winning earns slightly less; an upset with a weaker team earns slightly more.

    Ranked after 5 games. Until then you're listed as not ranked yet.
