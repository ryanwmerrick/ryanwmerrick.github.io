    How rankings work

    Overall is an Elo rating. Everyone starts at 1000. Before each game, the two players' Elo ratings are compared to predict who should win. Beat the prediction and you gain Elo; fall short and you lose it. So beating someone rated higher earns more than beating someone rated lower.

    New players move faster. How much one game can move your rating is called K. For your first 20 games K is 32, so you reach your real level quickly. After 20 games it drops to 20, so settled ratings don't jump around and are hard to pad. Example: between two equal players, a 1–0 win is worth +16 in your first 20 games and +10 after. When a new player plays a settled one, each moves by their own K.

    Margin counts a little. A regulation win is worth more the bigger the margin: 1 + 0.25 × ln(margin), up to 1.5×. A 2-goal win counts 1.17×, a 3-goal win 1.27×, a 5-goal win 1.40×. An OT or shootout win counts as 0.75 of a win with no margin bonus, so the loser still keeps a bit.

    No farming weaker players. Good players usually beat weaker players by a lot, so the margin bonus is trimmed when the favourite wins big and boosted when the underdog does. Blow out someone 200 Elo below you and the bonus is about 8% smaller; do it to someone 200 Elo above you and it's about 10% bigger. That keeps the payoff for only playing weaker players small.

    Teams count a little too. Every NHL team is in one of four tiers based on ESPN's 2026-27 preseason power rankings, adjusted by the group. For the prediction only, the tier's bonus is added to the Elo of whoever uses that team: Tier 1 +30, Tier 2 +10, Tier 3 −10, Tier 4 −30. A 1000-rated player using a Tier 1 team is treated as 1030. Winning with a stronger team earns slightly less; an upset with a weaker team earns slightly more. No team logged counts as +0.

    Home ice follows our results. For the prediction only, the home player gets extra Elo. It started at +25 and moves toward what our own games show, trusting our results a little more with every game (it always stays between 0 and +50). Right now it's +25, so between two equal players home is about a 54% favourite. Winning at home earns slightly less and winning away earns slightly more.

    These team and home bonuses are never added to anyone's actual rating. They only shape the prediction.

    Ranked after 5 games against at least 3 different opponents. Until then you're listed as not ranked yet, with your progress shown. Your rating still counts from your first game; this only decides who appears on the Overall leaderboard.

    Update, Oct 1, 2026. Three changes to make ratings calmer and fairer:

    1. Big wins count for less. A 3–0 win used to count as about 1.5 wins. Now it's about 1.3, and no win counts as more than 1.5.

    2. Ratings settle down after 20 games. After your 20th game, each game moves your rating about a third less. New players still find their level fast.

    3. Home ice follows our results. The home bonus started at +25 and now adjusts toward what our games actually show. With our games so far it's about +20.

    Every past game was recalculated with the new rules, so ratings shifted a little (less than 20 points), but the order of the standings didn't change.
